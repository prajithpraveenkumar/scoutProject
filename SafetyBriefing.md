# 🦅 Safety Briefing: Salt River Cleanup

**September 19, 2026 at 7:15 AM | Rio Salado Parking Lot**

## What to Wear
- Safety Vest
- Heavy-Duty Gloves
- Closed-Toe Shoes
- Hat & Sunscreen

## Watch Out For
- **Sharp Objects** - Wear gloves, use picker tools
- **Water** - Buddy system, stay shallow
- **Heavy Items** - Get help lifting
- **Heat** - Drink water every 20 minutes
- **Vehicles** - Wear vest, stay alert
- **Wildlife** - Don't bother them, tell a leader

## While Working
- Work with a buddy (never alone)
- Drink water often-dehydration is not fun
- Take breaks every 45 minutes
- Tell a leader if someone gets hurt
- Wash hands before eating
- Wear gloves at all times

## If Hurt
- Small cut → Wash and bandage
- Bad bleeding → Apply pressure, call 911
- Heat sick → Shade, water, rest
- Serious → Stop work, call 911

## Key Rules
1. Never work alone
2. Wear all gear
3. Tell a leader if unsafe
4. Drink water & take breaks
5. No fooling around
6. Listen to leaders
7. Don't touch unknown stuff
8. Stay in cleanup areas
