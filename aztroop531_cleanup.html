⚜️ How Can Scout Help?

Scouts can turn regular camping, hiking, fishing, and outdoor activities into opportunities to protect our waterways.
Keep Arizona's lakes, rivers, streams, and outdoor spaces clean!

Scouts can make it part of their next adventure. Scouts don't need to wait for a special cleanup event. Add a short trash-collection activity to your next camping trip, hike, fishing trip, service project, or outdoor adventure.

🌵 What Is the Trash Cleanup Project?

The AZ Community Science Alliance encourages people to help clean up trash around Arizona's waterways. Trash can attract wildlife and contribute to pollution near our water. By picking up litter and properly disposing of it, Scouts can help protect clean water, wildlife, and the places we enjoy outdoors. Learn more

✅ Steps to Participate in the Trash Cleanup Project

1

📍 Choose a Location

Look for a safe area near a lake, river, stream, trail, campsite, or other outdoor area where trash has collected.

2

📋 Plan Your Cleanup

Cleanups can be done individually or with a group. Include a cleanup as part of a camping trip, service project, hike, or conservation activity.

3

🧤 Bring Your Supplies

Use gloves, trash bags, buckets, and trash grabbers when available. The AZ Community Science Alliance can provide cleanup supplies to support participating volunteers.

4

🗑️ Collect the Trash

Pick up litter around the selected area. Stay on safe surfaces, avoid hazardous materials, and never handle anything that could be dangerous.

5

♻️ Sort & Dispose

Separate recyclable materials when appropriate and place other trash in proper disposal containers. Leave the area cleaner than you found it.

6

📝 Record What You Find

When participating in a community science activity, record information about the cleanup and the types of trash collected. This information can help scientists and communities understand environmental conditions.
